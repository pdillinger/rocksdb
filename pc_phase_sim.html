Periodic-compaction preferred-phase: badness

Real DBs (40), periodic_compaction_seconds N=12s, real periodic-task scheduler, per-DB listeners recording every compaction (periodic vs write-driven). Scenarios: pure periodic, natural writes (full compaction every 0.75N), and random write bursts (~every 1.5N) with a recovery-rate sweep.

Badness is essentially the fraction of compaction work that is ideally unnecessary (over-compaction) plus a penalty for late compactions — i.e., what you pay in extra total work to spread the work out. Formally: amortized realized cost / minimum-necessary (one per N per DB) − 1. Each compaction amortizes at 1/N over the next N; an early replacement by a periodic compaction charges the un-amortized remainder immediately (over-compaction); going overdue past N is charged double (lateness); early replacement by a write (the premise workload / seed) is free. So 0 = steady, and both “never compact” and “compact twice as often as needed” approach 1. Default recovery is now 33%.
